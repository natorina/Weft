# Weft privacy policy

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

Weft does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data.

**What Weft reads.** When you click the Weft icon on a store page, or visit a store where you turned on "Run automatically", Weft reads that page and opens that store's own product pages in the background to find their fabric composition and country of origin. It does not read pages on any other site.

**What Weft stores.** Your settings (for example, which materials you're looking for) and the fabric results for products you've viewed are saved in your browser's extension storage, on your device only. Fabric results are deleted after a week. Removing the extension deletes all of it.

**What Weft sends.** Nothing. Weft has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. The only network requests it makes are to the store you're browsing, for that store's product pages.

**Permissions.**
- *activeTab* and *scripting*: run Weft on the tab where you click the icon.
- *storage*: save your settings and cached results on your device.
- *Optional site access*: only requested, one store at a time, if you turn on "Run automatically" for that store. You can turn it off anytime.

Questions: open an issue on this project's GitHub page.
